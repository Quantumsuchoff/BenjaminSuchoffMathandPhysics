$$\\Phi(\\mathbf{r}) = -\\sum_{i=1}^{N} \\frac{GM_i}{\\|\\mathbf{r} - \\mathbf{r}_i\\|}$$

Step 2 — Apply the Gradient Operation

The gravitational acceleration field is the negative gradient of the potential:

$$\\mathbf{g}(\\mathbf{r}) = -\\nabla\\Phi = -\\frac{GM}{r^2}\\hat{r}$$

$$\\ddot{\\mathbf{r}} = \\mathbf{g}(\\mathbf{r}) + \\frac{1}{m}\\mathbf{F}_{con}$$
